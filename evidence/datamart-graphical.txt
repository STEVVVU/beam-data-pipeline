Sales datamart: transformed tables
Entity–relationship diagram for the tables emitted by the Beam normalization graph. The same schema is
used by the local report checks and the configured BigQuery sink. PK means primary key; FK means foreign
key.

                                quantity      INT64




   Each customer can place many orders; each product can appear in many orders. Each order references
exactly one customer and one product. The arrows point from each parent to its child table. A parent can
have zero or many orders.

Integrity boundary. These are logical relationships checked by reports.py and sql/bigquery.sql, not
automatically enforced BigQuery foreign-key constraints. The fixture intentionally contains one orphan
customer reference; the check reports it, and inner-join reports exclude that order. Duplicate or conflicting
source keys are resolved or rejected before this reporting stage.

Money. price cents is the normalized integer-cent value, not the original raw price string. Revenue
multiplies it by the order quantity with explicit numeric-range checks. No cloud execution is implied by this
schema diagram.
