\section*{Sales datamart: transformed tables}
\begin{tabular}{|l|l|l|}\hline
Table & Field & Type / key \\ \hline
customers\_Dataflow & customer\_id & STRING, primary key \\
 & name, region & STRING \\ \hline
products\_Dataflow & product\_id & STRING, primary key \\
 & name, category & STRING \\
 & price\_cents & INT64, cents \\ \hline
orders\_Dataflow & order\_id & STRING, primary key \\
 & customer\_id & STRING, foreign key to customers \\
 & product\_id & STRING, foreign key to products \\
 & order\_date & DATE \\
 & quantity & INT64 \\ \hline
\end{tabular}

Each customer can place many orders; each product can appear in many orders.
Each order references exactly one customer and one product. Foreign-key checks
identify unresolved references before reports are interpreted.
